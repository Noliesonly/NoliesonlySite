The world behind the songs

Fairy Tail / WolfSyde

The Fairy Tail is the sensitive side: magic, whimsy, beauty, softness, and the courage to stay open. The WolfSyde is the haunted side: rough edges, survival instincts, grief, grit, and the parts of growing up that leave marks.

Fairy Tail Softness as signal

Magic, memory, inner beauty, chosen family, self-expression, and the fragile parts worth protecting.

WolfSyde Grit as survival

Pressure, conflict, haunted streets, social tension, and the refusal to stop moving forward.

Pretty The voice in the center

A shifting face, a fixed signal, and the lead voice of the songs for now.
